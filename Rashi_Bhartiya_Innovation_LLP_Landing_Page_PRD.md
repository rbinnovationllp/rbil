# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Rashi Bhartiya Innovation LLP — Global Company Landing Website

**Document version:** 1.0  
**Prepared for:** Rashi Bhartiya Innovation LLP  
**Website type:** Corporate landing website and product gateway  
**Primary audience:** Global users, government departments, schools, institutions, investors, partners, NGOs, vendors, and the general public

---

## 1. Product Vision

Build a modern, trustworthy, visually attractive, and mobile-first corporate website for **Rashi Bhartiya Innovation LLP** that introduces the company worldwide and provides direct access to its four production products:

1. **EaseTalk** — <https://easetalk.in>
2. **Syllabus Synk** — <https://syllabus-synk.in>
3. **MAMAAI** — <https://mamaai.in>
4. **SabSewa Local** — <https://sabsewa.in>

The website must position the company as a purpose-driven innovation organisation that identifies genuine human difficulties through research and analysis and converts them into practical, accessible, and scalable digital solutions.

### Primary brand statement

> **We Research Real-Life Pain Points and Build Practical Solutions That Improve Everyday Life.**

This message must appear as a bold visual stamp or trust badge in the hero section and should be reinforced throughout the website.

### Suggested supporting statement

> From assistive communication and future-ready education to intelligent family nutrition and hyperlocal services, our innovations are built around people—not technology for technology’s sake.

---

## 2. Business Objectives

The website must:

- Establish Rashi Bhartiya Innovation LLP as the parent company behind all four products.
- Communicate the company’s research-led, people-first mission within five seconds of page load.
- Help visitors understand the problem solved by each product quickly.
- Direct users to the appropriate live product through prominent calls to action.
- Generate enquiries from government departments, schools, institutions, investors, partners, NGOs, and potential collaborators.
- Build confidence through transparent product information, founder story, company details, security commitments, policies, and contact information.
- Support future products without requiring a complete redesign.
- Present an Indian innovation company with global relevance and ambition.

- Inclusive

- Our Products
- Research & Innovation
- About Us

**Main headline:**

> **Transforming Everyday Challenges into Meaningful Digital Solutions**

**Supporting copy:**

> Rashi Bhartiya Innovation LLP researches real human needs and develops accessible, affordable, and intelligent solutions for communication, education, family wellness, and hyperlocal services.

### 6.5 How innovation begins

Heading: **We Do Not Start With Technology. We Start With People.**

1. **Listen** — Identify recurring difficulties faced by individuals, families, institutions, and communities.
2. **Research & Analyse** — Study root causes, existing solutions, affordability barriers, and user context.
3. **Design & Validate** — Develop practical solutions and test them with relevant users and domain experts.
4. **Improve & Scale** — Use feedback and evidence to strengthen the product for wider adoption.

Include a CTA: **Discover Our Research Approach**.

Avoid publishing numerical impact counters until verifiable data is available. The content management system should support verified metrics later.

## 9. Contact and Lead Management

### Contact form fields

- Full name
- Organisation
- Country
- Email
- Phone/WhatsApp with country code (optional)
- Area of interest: EaseTalk, Syllabus Synk, MAMAAI, SabSewa Local, Government Pilot, Partnership, Investment, Media, Other
- Message
- Consent checkbox linked to Privacy Policy

### Functional requirements

- Validate all fields on client and server.
- Protect forms using rate limiting, honeypot and/or privacy-conscious bot protection.
- Send acknowledgement to the visitor.
- Route the enquiry to the correct official company inbox.
- Store lead source, selected product, submission time, consent, and status securely.
- Never display private email credentials or API keys in frontend code.
- Provide an admin-export option or connect to an approved CRM in a later phase.

The public site must not expose the four product support inboxes as general enquiry destinations unless the company specifically approves this.

## 10. Visual and UX Requirements

### Design direction

- Premium but warm—not a generic corporate technology template.
- Use generous white space, bold typography, subtle gradients, and purpose-led imagery.
- Give each product a distinct accent colour while maintaining one parent brand system.
- Use light motion to reveal content and explain the four-product ecosystem.
- Avoid heavy animations, auto-playing audio, flashing elements, and excessive carousels.

### Suggested colour roles

- Deep blue or indigo: trust and technology
- Saffron/orange accent: energy and Indian origin
- Green: positive human and community impact
- Warm neutral background: approachability

Final colours must meet WCAG contrast requirements and must not imitate national emblems or imply government endorsement.

### Responsive behaviour

- Mobile-first layouts for common Android screen sizes
- Tablet and desktop adaptations
- Minimum 44×44 px touch targets
- Product cards stack vertically on smaller screens
- No horizontal scrolling at 320 px viewport width
- Hero headline and stamp must remain readable without zooming

---

## 11. Accessibility Requirements

Target **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** where practical.

- Full keyboard navigation
- Visible focus states
- Semantic headings and landmarks
- Meaningful alternative text for images
- Captions and transcripts for videos
- Form labels and understandable validation errors
- Screen-reader-friendly navigation
- Respect reduced-motion preferences
- Do not communicate meaning using colour alone
- Language attributes for English and Hindi content
- Accessibility feedback contact

Accessibility is particularly important because EaseTalk is an assistive product; the parent website must reflect the same commitment.

---

## 12. Functional and Technical Requirements

- Use a modern, secure, SEO-friendly framework capable of static or server-rendered pages.
- Provide a lightweight content management method for text, product status, URLs, FAQs, and verified metrics.
- Store product URLs in one configuration object.
- Check external product links automatically during deployment and through scheduled monitoring.
- Open external products safely using `rel="noopener noreferrer"` where applicable.
- Optimise images using modern formats, responsive sizing, and lazy loading.
- Use HTTPS throughout.
- Add secure headers, dependency monitoring, input sanitisation, and server-side validation.
- Maintain separate development, staging, and production environments.
- Do not copy private user information from any product into the corporate site.
- Provide a branded 404 page and graceful error states.
- Add uptime monitoring for the corporate site and product links.
- Use analytics only after appropriate consent and policy implementation.

### Performance targets

- Lighthouse performance target: 90 or higher on representative mobile testing
- Largest Contentful Paint: under 2.5 seconds at the 75th percentile where feasible
- Cumulative Layout Shift: below 0.1
- Interaction to Next Paint: below 200 ms where feasible
- Compressed initial page payload and no unnecessary third-party scripts

## 14. Privacy, Legal, and Trust Requirements

- Publish accurate Privacy Policy and Terms of Use reviewed for applicable jurisdictions.
- Obtain consent before non-essential cookies or tracking.
- Explain how contact-form data is used and retained.
- Provide a method to request correction or deletion of enquiry data.
- Do not use government logos, seals, partner logos, testimonials, awards, or media claims without written permission.
- Do not present wellness or meal-planning information as medical diagnosis or treatment.
- Clearly distinguish company information from the separate terms and privacy policies of each product.
- Display “Coming Soon,” “Pilot,” “Beta,” or “Production” status accurately for every product.
- Obtain permission for all photographs, videos, illustrations, testimonials, and case studies.

## 15. Analytics and Success Measurement

Track privacy-respectful events such as:

- Product card views
- Clicks on each **Open Product** button
- Demo and partnership requests
- Contact-form completion rate
- Traffic source and country at an aggregated level
- Language selection
- Returning visitors
- Broken product-link events

### Initial success indicators

- Visitors understand the company purpose and four product areas.
- Product click-through rates increase over time.
- Qualified institutional and partnership enquiries are received.
- Mobile visitors can reach a product in no more than three taps.
- No critical accessibility, privacy, security, or broken-link issue remains at launch.

Do not publish internal analytics publicly without review.

## 18. Acceptance Criteria

The website is ready for launch only when:

- All four products are visible on the Home page and have working links to their correct production URLs.
- The research-and-pain-point brand stamp appears prominently above the fold.
- Every advertised feature is verified or accurately marked by status.
- The website works across current mobile, tablet, and desktop browsers.
- Navigation, forms, and key journeys pass keyboard and screen-reader checks.
- English content has been proofread and approved by the company.
- Legal pages, consent controls, and contact-data handling are implemented.
- Page speed, metadata, sitemap, structured data, and social previews are tested.
- Forms route successfully and do not leak secrets or personal data.
- Broken-link, security, and accessibility checks show no critical issues.
- A non-technical administrator can update product text, status, and URLs safely.
- Backup, rollback, monitoring, and ownership handover are documented.

Build the website from this PRD as a polished corporate experience, not merely a collection of four product links. The parent-company purpose must remain dominant: **Rashi Bhartiya Innovation LLP studies real human pain points and builds practical, inclusive solutions through research, analysis, and responsible innovation.**

Create reusable components and a central product-data configuration so future innovations can be added without restructuring the site.
